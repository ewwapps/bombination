Bombination Privacy Policy

Effective September 26, 2026

In short: Bombination does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and makes no network connections.

Information we collect

None. Bombination runs entirely on your device. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it has no sign-in or account system.

Network access

The game makes no internet connections. All game logic, including the computer opponent, runs locally on your device, and the game works fully offline.

Data stored on your device

Bombination does not save anything to your device's storage. A game in progress exists only while the app is open and is discarded when the app closes.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

Bombination contains no advertising, no analytics tools, and no third-party software development kits. It does not track you across apps or websites.

Information collected by Apple

Apple may collect information when you download or use apps from the App Store, under Apple's Privacy Policy. If you have chosen in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide me with aggregated, anonymous statistics such as crash reports. These do not identify you personally.

Children's privacy

Bombination is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds a feature that handles data, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
